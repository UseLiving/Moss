The MossFlywheel

Two loops that push each other. Inside the game, every harvest makes the next one bigger. Around the game, every $MOSS players spend feeds burns, prizes and stakers, which brings in more players.

Loop 1 · the snowball

Every harvest pays for a bigger one.

This loop already runs in the game. The numbers are the game's real ones.

STEP 1Farm20 $MOSS → 1 glowcap sporeClear the field, till and plant. Energy limits how much you can do each day.
STEP 2Harvest~40 per glowcapWatered crops grow overnight. Glowcaps are ready in 3 days, moonmorels in 8 at ~270 each.
STEP 3Sell smartprices move nightlyShip at the close or sell on the Exchange. Selling into a rally can add 30% or more.
STEP 4Reinvestcan 900 · pack 1,200 · tonic 1,500Bigger can, more bag space, more energy and pricier seeds all raise what you can produce per day.
STEP 5Stake the restRoot Vault · 1.2% a nightIdle coins compound. Withdraw for the next big seed order. Then back to step 1, bigger.
Loop 2 · the flywheel

Spending in the game feeds the token.

This connects $MOSS to a real token. The rewards come from what players spend, not from printing more.

STEP 1Players joinA cozy game people want to play. Free to start, with a wallet only needed to trade or claim prizes.
STEP 2They spend $MOSSPremium spores, extra land plots, tool upgrades, golden cosmetics, guild halls. Real reasons to hold and use the token.
STEP 3Root Treasury fillsEvery $MOSS spent in a sink goes to the treasury. The game also adds its Exchange fees (5%).
STEP 4It splits four waysBurn, a season prize pool, real yield for Vault stakers and a slice for building. See the split below.
STEP 5Scarcer and richerBurns shrink supply. Prize pools grow with activity. Staking yield rises with spending, not with inflation.
STEP 6More playersBigger prizes and a visible burn counter are reasons to tell friends. New players spend, and the wheel turns faster.

Faucets and sinks

More has to leave than arrive.

A faucet creates $MOSS. A sink removes it or moves it to the treasury. If faucets beat sinks for long, the token inflates and the wheel slows.

Faucets CAPPED

  • Selling crops, gems and forageMain incomePaid from a daily emission budget that shrinks 10% each season.
  • Story goals and villager requestsOne-timeFixed rewards. Never repeatable for profit.
  • Season prizesFrom treasuryRecycled from spending, so no new tokens.
  • Vault yieldFrom treasuryReal yield only. If nobody spends, yield falls toward zero.

Sinks TO ADD

  • Premium sporesRare cropsMoonmorels and seasonal spores only sold for $MOSS.
  • Land plotsBigger farmExpand the field. Price rises with each plot you own.
  • Tool and house upgradesTime saversSprinklers, copper and gold tools, a bigger cap house.
  • CosmeticsStatusCap colors, pets, farm decorations. Pure flex, no power.
  • Exchange fee5% of salesAlready in the game. Routes to the treasury.
  • Vault early exit2% feeLeaving within 7 nights pays remaining stakers.

Keeping it honest

Flywheels spin both ways.

The same loop that snowballs up can snowball down if players leave. These rules keep it pointed the right way.

Fun comes firstThe wheel only turns if people want to play. If the only reason to play is the token, it stalls the moment the price drops.
Real yield onlyVault and prize rewards come out of what players spend. Nothing is promised. Quiet weeks pay less, and the page should say so.
Skill-based prizesSeason prizes go to top farmers by a mix of harvest, trading profit and quests, with a cap per wallet and anti-bot checks.
Shrinking emissionsThe daily emission budget for selling crops drops each season, so early farmers can't flood the supply forever.
No power you can only buyUpgrades save time, cosmetics show off. A free player can still reach the top of the season board.
Everything publicShow the treasury, total burned and the prize pool live, on a page anyone can check. Trust is part of the wheel.
